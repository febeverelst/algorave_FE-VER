## What is this project?

This project started as part of a __*creative development*__ course where I was given the freedom to explore a technology in depth through a personal passion project.

Instead of working on a typical school assignment, the idea was to choose something I’m genuinely interested in and really spend time with it. The focus isn’t on building something for a client, but on experimenting, researching, and __*pushing myself beyond*__ what I already know as a developer.

For me, that became algorave and live coding.

Throughout this project, I’ve been teaching myself new tools and ways of working that go beyond the regular curriculum. A big part of that process is figuring things out on my own, running into problems, and slowly building understanding through trial and error. Not everything works, and that’s kind of the point.

This part blog exists to document that process as honestly as possible. It’s not just about showing results, but about keeping track of what __*I try, what fails, what improves,*__ and how my thinking evolves over time.

Even though it started as a school project, it’s slowly becoming something more personal. It’s a space where I explore how code, music, and performance come together, and where I challenge myself to grow further than what I already know.
